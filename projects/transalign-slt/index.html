TransAlign-SLT

Complementary Temporal and Semantic Supervision for Gloss-Free Sign Language Translation

Pallapu Mohan Krishna · Uppala Sanjay Kumar · M. K. Bhuyan · Debanga Raj Neog

Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati · Mehta Family School of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence

GitHub repository ↗Contact

Overview

Learning sign language translation from paired videos and spoken-language translations, without gloss or pseudo-gloss annotations, requires both temporal organization and visual–language semantic correspondence. TransAlign-SLT combines auxiliary TranslationCTC as a coarse temporal prior with Masked Entropic Optimal Transport (MOT) for soft semantic alignment. A corpus-adapted, frozen BERT encoder provides training-time semantic representations, while the main inference path remains compact.

Fully gloss-free

Trained directly from sign videos and translations without gloss annotations.

TranslationCTC

Auxiliary temporal supervision with coefficient decaying from 0.5 to 0.1.

Semantic alignment

Masked optimal transport between visual features and word-level contextual representations.

Method

Main inference path

Sign video→ResNet18→HMTF→BiLSTM→Attention + GRU→Translation
Temporal training supervision

TranslationCTC on HMTF and BiLSTM features.

Semantic training supervision

Frozen corpus-adapted BERT + word pooling + MOT.

BERT and MOT are training-only. Optional post-hoc TranslationCTC reranking evaluates completed hypotheses; it is separate from autoregressive beam expansion.

HMTF adapts the temporal feature extraction architecture of GPGN without gloss-supervised pretrained weights.

Related inspiration and resources
